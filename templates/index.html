Educational Intelligence

Pollution Explained: Science of the Urban Atmosphere

Master the physics, chemistry, and meteorology governing the air you breathe every second.

PM2.5: The Microscopic Intruder

Particulate Matter ≤ 2.5 microns (1/30th the width of a human hair). It bypasses nasal hairs, settling deep inside lung alveoli and passing directly into the bloodstream.

Defense: Cloth masks offer zero electrostatic hold; only certified N95 / FFP2 respirators filter PM2.5.

PM10: Coarse Inhalable Dust

Particles between 2.5 and 10 microns, consisting of road resuspension, construction debris, pollen, and tyre wear. Trapped primarily in the upper respiratory tract.

Defense: Wet mopping and damp microfiber dusting prevent domestic re-suspension.

NO2: The Combustion Marker

Nitrogen Dioxide is emitted at high temperatures by internal combustion engines. It irritates airways and is the primary precursor to ground-level ozone.

Defense: Electrified urban rail transit generates zero roadside tailpipe NO₂.

Ground-Level Ozone (O3)

Formed on hot sunny days through photochemical reactions between NOx and VOCs. Causes acute chest tightness during outdoor workouts.

Defense: Schedule outdoor workouts in early morning or after sunset on high-ozone summer days.

Thermal Inversion & Boundary Layers

During winter nights, the ground cools rapidly, trapping vehicle emissions under cold air like a sealed lid until midday sun elevates the boundary layer.

Defense: Ventilate indoor spaces between 11:30 AM and 3:00 PM when the inversion lid lifts.

US EPA AQI vs EAQI Standards

Air Quality Indices convert multi-pollutant concentrations into a single number based on piecewise breakpoints. PRANVYRA uses the US EPA AQI standard (40 CFR Part 58).

Defense: Always monitor the dominant sub-index rather than an unweighted chemical average.
